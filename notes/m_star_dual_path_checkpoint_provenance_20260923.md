# M-giant dual-path checkpoint provenance (2026-09-23)

All eighteen M-giant dual-path targets in
`notes/ledgers/m_star_dual_path_validation_v1.json` were initialized by the
v4 checkpoints `artifacts/m_star_emulator_mgiant_v4/` (corpus
`m_star_cool_corpus_mgiant_v2`).

The dual-path products store the reconstructed start atmosphere as
`iter_0000.npz` but not the checkpoint that predicted it, and the v4
revalidation of 2026-09-11 rewrote the twelve stop-rule emulator arms in place
(`logs/mstar_stop_rule_v1_validate_v4.log`). Physical reconstruction holds
`(m, T)` fixed, so each stored seed equals the coordinate-wise median of the
three seed predictions. Repeating that prediction with the v3 and v4
checkpoints, without running the solver:

| sample | stored seeds | v4 max difference | v3 min difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| stop-rule twelve (`emulator/`) | 12 | ≤ 1.3e-15 | ≥ 6.4e-4 |
| resolve-undetermined five (`emulator60/`) | 5 | ≤ 1.3e-15 | ≥ 2.2e-2 |
| sixty-budget six (`emulator/`) | 6 | ≤ 8.9e-16 | ≥ 7.8e-3 |

Differences are the larger of the maximum relative temperature difference and
the maximum `log10 m` difference in dex. Every stored seed matches v4 to
floating-point rounding; none matches v3.

Record: `results/m_star_dual_path_checkpoint_provenance_20260923/provenance.json`,
regenerated by

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m \
  experiments.reduced_state_emulator.m_star_dual_path_checkpoint_provenance
```

The default `DEFAULT_CHECKPOINT_DIR` in `m_star_stop_rule_v1.py` still names
v3; the v4 runs passed the checkpoint directory explicitly.

## Budget history of the eighteen targets

The v4 checkpoint is common to all eighteen targets; the emulator iteration
budget is not.

- Stop-rule twelve: preregistered emulator budget 30. Under v4, 7/12 pass and
  5/12 are undetermined (`notes/m_star_stop_rule_v1_revalidation_closeout_20260911.md`).
- The 60-iteration budget was adopted after those twelve results were seen
  (`notes/m_star_resolve_undetermined_closeout_20260912.md`). The twelve are
  therefore development evidence for the 60-iteration configuration.
- Sixty-budget six: preregistered with the 60-iteration configuration before
  any solve; 5/6 stabilize within 60 and one at 90
  (`notes/m_star_sixty_budget_v1_closeout_20260913.md`,
  `notes/m_star_gravity_walk_v1_closeout_20260913.md`). These six are the
  prospective test of that configuration.

Neither the twelve nor the six appear in the v3 or v4 training corpora. The
v4 corpus adds corner truth rows at `g1.5 [M/H]=-0.5`, `t3100` and `t3200`,
beside the stop-rule point `t3150 g1.5 [M/H]=-0.5`; the v4 revalidation
records that this point still did not stabilize within 30 iterations.
